Top LPs for NDM

STAR

  • Situation → Task → I Actions → Result (+ Learning)
  • ~2 min; invite follow-ups
  • Result needs baseline → delta

Ops triage

  1. Impact — who, how much, since when?
  2. Mitigate — restore customers first
  3. Scope — failure domain / blast radius
  4. Change correlate — deploys, peers, fiber
  5. Dive deep — telemetry, not guesses
  6. Durable fix — stop recurrence

Green ≠ healthy

  • Probe miss path/region/cohort
  • Admin-up without traffic/errors

Change & capacity

  • Peer review · canary · rollback · owner
  • No overlapping failure-domain changes
  • Forecast: peaks + growth + repair margin
  • Lead indicators → capacity before demand
  • Emergency change = fast + audited

Physics napkin

  • 1 TB @ 10 Mbps ≈ 9.3 days
  • Always convert to same units (Mb)
